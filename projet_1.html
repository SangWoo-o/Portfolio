Mechatronics Project: Walking Quadruped Robot

During my second year at ELISA, my team and I Designed and fabricated a walking quadruped robot. The project features 3D CAD modeling of custom adaptation components, a regulated power supply system, and a static balancing algorithm written in Arduino C++.

💻 Arduino C++ 📐 3DExperience CAD 🖨️ PLA 3D Printing
01 INITIAL STAGES

CAD Architecture and Assembly

Mechanical study and structural assembly the quadruped robot.

  • Mechanical Analysis: Drafted a numerical analysis report evaluating key mechanical and physical constraints.
  • 3D Structure: 3D-printed main chassis and 4 articulated legs using PLA but also a custom Arduino Nano mounting plate, a bearing spacer, and an integrated ON/OFF switch housing.
  • Actuation: Integration of 12 servomotors for dynamic joint control.
02 ELECTRONIC ARCHITECTURE

Electronics & Component Integration

Design of the power distribution circuit to drive the 12 servos and onboard hardware (gyroscope, battery system, LCD screen).

  • Power Management: Custom regulator circuit stepping down a 7.4V LiPo battery to 5V.
  • Sensors & Wiring: Soldering and wiring sensors to the expansion board connected to the Arduino controller.
03 • ROBOT PROGRAMMING

Arduino C++ Gait & Control Algorithms

Development of an Arduino C++ program to achieve active gyro-stabilized balancing and forward gait motion.

  • Mass Transfer: Dynamic center-of-mass shift algorithms to prevent tipping during movement.
  • Asymmetric Load Balancing: Effort amplitude compensation for the heavier rear section of the frame.
  • Joint Angle Mapping: Normalizing servo angle matrices for smooth, coordinated quadruped locomotion.

🛠️ Engineering Methodology & Problem Solving

  • Board Mounting Adapter: The original frame was designed for an Arduino Mega; we rapidly modeled and printed a custom adapter plate in 3DExperience to mount our Arduino Nano without delaying project milestones.
  • Voltage Drop Mitigation: Solved transient power dips caused by simultaneous current draws across 12 servos by reinforcing power distribution rails and isolating the I2C bus for the screen and gyroscope.
  • Mechanical Workaround: Designed and 3D-printed a custom shim washer to compensate for a missing bearing, ensuring uninterrupted assembly.
